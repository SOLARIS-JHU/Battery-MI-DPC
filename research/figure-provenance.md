## Caption summaries and interpretation

**Figure 2:** “Hybrid gradient approach for differentiable rainflow cycle counting.”

The five panels show an initial state-of-charge trajectory and cycle pairings, the exact gradient at extrema, the dense proxy gradient, their hybrid, and trajectory evolution. This explains the gradient construction; it should not be described as a fleet-dispatch benchmark or a measured field experiment. Suggested alt text: “Five panels show rainflow cycles on a state-of-charge trajectory, sparse exact gradients at extrema, dense proxy gradients, the combined hybrid gradient, and the trajectory evolving toward a flatter profile.”

**Figure 3, caption summary:** A sensitivity study over 50 trajectories examines daily rainflow cost as a function of λ. Panel (a) plots cost during optimization; panel (b) compares final costs across a detailed λ sweep. The complete original caption is at HTML `S4.F3` in the versioned source above.

Panel (a) uses a logarithmic dollar-cost axis and 300 iterations; panel (b) preserves the inset and uncertainty shading. Section IV-A describes a broad interior basin and uses λ = 0.5 as a robust default, rather than establishing a universal optimal mixing weight. Do not assign a statistical interpretation to the shading beyond what the source specifies. Suggested alt text: “Exact rainflow degradation cost over iterations for five exact-gradient weights, followed by a sweep of final dollar cost versus mixing weight with an enlarged view of the low-cost interior region.”

**Figure 4, caption summary:** Projection is illustrated with one summer day's trajectory, a histogram describing the size of infeasibilities, and their seasonal distribution. The complete original caption is at HTML `S4.F4` in the versioned source above.

The original axes use SOC in kWh, violation as percent of capacity, frequency, month, and infeasible days. The first panel shows one representative infeasible trajectory and its projected counterpart; it is not an optimality guarantee. The source's horizontal ticks in that panel are preserved without adding a missing axis label. Suggested alt text: “A representative state-of-charge trajectory before and after feasibility projection, a histogram of violation magnitudes as percent of capacity, and monthly counts of infeasible days.”

The v1 paper contains Figures 1–4 only. Its fleet-aggregation discussion is Section IV-G and Table III; there is no Figure 6 or separate fleet-aggregation plot in this version. No such plot has been synthesized for the website.
